Hatched blocks are scheduled classes from the course schedule, so those halls are not free at those times. Blocks with a chequered left edge are seminars from the seminar listing. Plain solid blocks are bookings the office has confirmed.
To ask for a hall, click the hours you want. A click takes the whole hour and dragging takes any stretch down to a quarter of an hour. Then press Book, say who it is for and what for, and you are handed a booking code. Get that to the office by any means and the hall is yours once they enter it. Nothing is held until then. Hours you have picked keep their place when you move to another day, so a course that meets on several days is one request and one code.
