Conservation Laws

Theory, Numerics, and Scientific Computing

Dates & Venue 16th–18th December, 2026

Department of Mathematics
Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore

Conference Goal

To bring together India's active research community in hyperbolic conservation laws with leading international experts, strengthen collaborations, expose students and early-career researchers to current developments, and build a cohesive national research community in this strategically important area.

Focal Themes

Focal Themes

Hyperbolic conservation laws constitute one of the central areas of modern applied mathematics, with deep connections to nonlinear partial differential equations, numerical analysis, and scientific computing. They provide the mathematical framework for modeling transport and wave propagation phenomena arising in fluid dynamics, traffic flow, multiphase flows, geophysical hazards, environmental sciences, and several other engineering and industrial applications. Recent developments in conservation laws and machine learning-based computational methods have further broadened the scope and impact of the subject.

  • Scalar and Systems of Hyperbolic Conservation Laws
  • Nonlocal Conservation Laws
  • Well-Posedness and Qualitative Theory
  • Numerical Methods (Finite Volume, Front Tracking, and Related Schemes)
  • Machine Learning-Based Algorithms for Conservation Laws
  • Current Trends and Emerging Research Directions
  • Industrial and Multidisciplinary Applications of Conservation Laws
