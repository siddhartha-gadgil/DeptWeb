Conservation Laws

Theory, Numerics, and Scientific Computing

Dates & Venue 16th–18th December, 2026

Department of Mathematics
Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore

Conference Goal

To bring together India's active research community in hyperbolic conservation laws with leading international experts, strengthen collaborations, expose students and early-career researchers to the research area and its current developments, and build a cohesive research community in this domain.

Focal Themes

Hyperbolic conservation laws provide the mathematical framework for modeling real-world phenomena such as wave propagation in fluid dynamics, traffic flow, crowd flow, geophysical hazards, environmental sciences, and several other engineering and industrial applications. Recent developments in conservation laws and their machine learning-based approximations have further broadened the impact of the subject. The conference will focus on the following major themes:

  • Well-Posedness and Qualitative Theory
  • Numerical Methods
  • Machine Learning-Based Algorithm
  • Industrial and Multidisciplinary Applications
