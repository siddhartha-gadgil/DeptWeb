Request an IISc email ID (new postdocs)

Fill in the details exactly as they should appear on your IISc account, and attach your joining report / joining memo as a PDF, signed by the Registrar, Deputy Registrar or Assistant Registrar (HR). The office forwards the request to IISc email support; a copy comes to you.
