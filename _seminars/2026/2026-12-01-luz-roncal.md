How does restricting the radii of circular averages to a fractal set affect their maximal estimates? This question connects classical problems in harmonic analysis with the
geometry of fractal sets and the smoothing properties of dispersive equations.

In these two lectures, I will introduce the circular maximal function and discuss how the size and local distribution of the dilation set influence its $L^p$–$L^q$ bounds.
After reviewing classical theory and the roles of Minkowski and Assouad dimensions, I will present recent joint work with Sanghyuk Lee, Feng Zhang and Shuijiang Zhao. We
establish previously open endpoint estimates for the fractal circular maximal function and obtain related local smoothing estimates for the wave propagator. I will explain
the connection between these problems and discuss the main ideas of the bilinear approach used in the proofs.

If time permits, I will also introduce Strichartz estimates for fractional Schrödinger equations over fractal time sets, highlighting how the geometry of the time set enters
the estimates. The latter is joint work with Jin Bong Lee and Sanghyuk Lee.
