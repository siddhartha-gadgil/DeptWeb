In 1981, Ronald C. Read used the classical deletion–contraction principle to obtain an explicit formula for the generalized chromatic polynomials of cycle graphs. In
this talk, I will present a new proof of Read's formula using a linear-algebraic approach. The key idea is to realize these polynomials as traces of certain linear
operators. This is a joint work with Dipnit Biswas.
