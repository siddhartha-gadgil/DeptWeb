Algebraic cycles and their associated invariants, such as Chow groups and motivic cohomology, play a fundamental role in understanding the geometry and arithmetic of
algebraic varieties. For smooth varieties over local fields, a central tool for relating these invariants to étale cohomology is the cycle class map, whose properties
are closely related to unramified cohomology and algebraic K-theory.

In this talk, we will study cycle class maps for smooth (not necessarily projective) surfaces over local fields and present new results concerning their injectivity under
suitable hypotheses. We will explain how these results are established by proving certain vanishing results for unramified cohomology, together with some exactness properties
of Gersten complexes in algebraic K-theory. We then apply these results to answer questions concerning the left and right kernels of the Brauer–Manin pairing for open
subvarieties of such surfaces. This talk is based on recent joint work with Amalendu Krishna.
